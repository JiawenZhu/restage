INSTRUCTION

Relight from a window camera-left; let the shadow side fall off naturally.

WHY

Overhead room light flattens the face and puts a shadow under the jaw that no phone video has. Window light from the side is what a real kitchen gives you at that hour.

CRITIC · PARTIAL, RETRIED ONCE

“First pass lit her like a beauty campaign — clean gradient on the cheek, no room left behind her. That is the opposite of the goal. Retried with the source pushed to one side and the background allowed to blow out slightly, which is what a real window does.”
