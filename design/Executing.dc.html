INSTRUCTION

Diffuse the key light and lift the shadow side by roughly one stop.

WHY

The hard-edged shadow under the bottle is the signature of a single studio strobe. Phone cameras in a room produce a wide, soft falloff. Softening it removes the second-strongest studio tell after the backdrop.
