INSTRUCTION

Introduce a hand entering from the lower right, fingers in contact with the bottle.

WHY

Contact is what separates a held object from a photographed one. The feed formats that outperform studio work almost always show a hand — it signals the product was used, not arranged. Entering from the lower right keeps the label unobstructed.

CRITIC · ACHIEVED

“The hand reads as holding rather than hovering — the fingertips deform slightly against the bottle and the contact shadow follows them. Skin tone sits within a stop of the surface light, so it belongs to the same room. The label stays fully legible.”

Judged against: does the product look held, not placed?
