DECOMPOSING THE GOAL

Six steps, in this order.

Read it before anything renders. Cut a step you disagree with.

1

Put her in a real room

A blank wall reads as a set. Kitchens are where this product is actually used.

2

Move to arm’s-length framing

Nobody films themselves on a tripod. The camera has to be a hand.

3

Light it from the window

Overhead light flattens the face. Side light is what a real kitchen gives at that hour.

Get the product to the lens

The bottle has to be legible in the first two seconds or the scroll wins.
